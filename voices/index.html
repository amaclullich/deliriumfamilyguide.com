What it was like

About these quotations

The quotations come from accounts published by the NHS and other health services, universities, charities and medical journals. Each one is copied word for word as published, including its spelling and punctuation. Follow the link under a quotation to read the full account.

Some quotations come from research interviews, where the people taking part are not named. Quotations from articles published under a Creative Commons licence show the licence beside the source.

The people quoted have no connection with this book and have not endorsed it. Their accounts describe the problems the book was written to help with. These include not knowing the word delirium, concerns that are not acted on, and delirium mistaken for dementia or mental illness.

Quotations checked against their sources on 24 September 2026. Editorial responsibility: Professor Alasdair MacLullich, Professor of Geriatric Medicine at the University of Edinburgh. To report an error, email alasdair@the4at.com.
