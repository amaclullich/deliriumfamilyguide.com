Delirium Family Guide

Medical and editorial policy

Last reviewed: 4 August 2026

Purpose

DeliriumFamilyGuide.com provides information about the forthcoming book Delirium (Acute Confusion): A Family Guide to Sudden Changes in Thinking and Memory. The website and the book have the same purpose: to help patients, families and carers understand delirium and respond to it. The book offers greater depth in a structured Kindle or print format that can be read in full or kept nearby for reference.

Authors

The guide is written by Professor Alasdair MacLullich and Dr Sharmella Summan. Professor MacLullich is Professor of Geriatric Medicine at the University of Edinburgh and developed the 4AT delirium assessment tool. Dr Summan is a doctor who has cared for people with delirium and experienced severe delirium herself while in intensive care.

Professor MacLullich has editorial responsibility for medical information on this website. His credentials and research record can be checked through his University of Edinburgh profile, research profile, ORCID record and personal website.

Medical information

The information on this website is general and educational. It is not individual medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it does not replace assessment or care from a healthcare professional.

Sudden confusion needs prompt medical assessment. If this is happening now, read what to do now on Delirium Support and follow the urgent or emergency advice for the place where the person is.

How the content is prepared

The website gives a short account of the book and does not reproduce the book's full explanations or references. Medical statements are checked for consistency with established delirium guidance and the authors' clinical, research and lived experience. Substantive reviews are dated, and material factual errors will be corrected.

Independence

This is an independent publication website maintained by Delirium Support Press. It is not an official website of the University of Edinburgh, the NHS or NHS Lothian, and those organisations are not responsible for its content.

This website provides information about a forthcoming book by its authors. It currently carries no advertising, affiliate links or paid medical sponsorship. Linked websites have their own editorial responsibility.

Corrections and contact

Factual corrections, accessibility problems and questions about this policy may be sent to alasdair@the4at.com.
